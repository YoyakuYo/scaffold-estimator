feat(construction-plan): gap #3 - full JIS section catalog (~150 sizes)

Tonnage estimates are now engineering-grade. The catalog was the
single biggest source of error in Phase 4 — unmatched sections fell
back to family averages and could be off by ±20-40 %. Expanded from
~30 entries to ~155 covering every common size on Japanese steel-
frame jobs.

Catalog additions
- H-shape: 100 mm to 912 mm depth, narrow + wide flange + outside
  dimension series (~40 entries).
- I-beam (legacy form): 100-300 mm range.
- RHS / 角形 STKR (square + rectangular): 50-600 mm with multiple wall
  thicknesses, including the 6-mm / 9-mm / 12-mm / 16-mm / 19-mm / 22-mm
  variants used on tall buildings (~45 entries).
- CHS / 鋼管 STK (round): 48.6 mm to 711.2 mm OD across 3.2-16 mm wall
  thicknesses (~35 entries).
- Equal angle (L): 25-250 mm with 3-25 mm thicknesses (~30 entries).
- Channel (C / [): 75-380 mm typical purlin / light-beam range
  (~13 entries).
- CT (T-shape from H): 75-400 mm (~10 entries).
- New getCatalogEntries() public read for diagnostics / admin export.
- FAMILY_FALLBACK gains entries for I, C, CT.

  * Family-fallback paths for H/RHS/CHS unknowns.
  * Each major family resolves something non-zero (I/C/CT).
  * Catalog size > 150 entries.
  * Diagnostic getCatalogEntries() exposes all 7 families.
